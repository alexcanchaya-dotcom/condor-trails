Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 18 November 2026

These Terms & Conditions explain how Condor Trails works with you, and how bookings are made with our Peru operating partner. Condor Trails is a trading name of a business owned and operated by Aleksander Canchaya, based in Dublin, Ireland. Please read these terms carefully before you enquire or book.

Condor Trails is the English-language sales front for a Lima family tour operator run by the founder's parents. We introduce travellers from English-speaking countries — including Ireland, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand — to trips in Peru and related journeys the operator already offers. We help you plan in English. We are not the company you book or pay for the trip itself.

Contact Condor Trails: info@condortrails.com · +353 85 760 4985 · Dublin, Ireland

In these Terms & Conditions:

  • "We", "us", "our" or "Condor Trails" refers to Condor Trails, operated by Aleksander Canchaya, Dublin, Ireland.
  • "You", "your" refers to the person making an enquiry or the lead traveller.
  • "Operator" means Maria Kralewska Turismo y Aventura S.A.C. (also known as MK Tours and MK Turismo), a tour operator established in Lima, Peru. Public details confirmed from the Operator's own sites and official tax records are set out in section 16. Marketing sites include tourinca.com and mariakralewska.com.pe.
  • "Booking" means a confirmed travel arrangement between you and the Operator, not a contract with Condor Trails for the performance of the trip.
  • "Written quote" means the itinerary, price and booking conditions sent to you in writing before you confirm a booking with the Operator.
  • "Departure date" means the date on which your trip is scheduled to commence.

2. Booking & Payment

2.1 Our role

Condor Trails introduces you to the Operator and sells on the Operator's behalf as an English-language sales front. We take your enquiry, help you shape a trip around what the Operator already offers, and remain available in English as your point of contact. The booking contract for the trip is with the Operator. The Operator runs the trip and is the company you pay.

Condor Trails may earn a fee from the Operator for bookings we introduce. That fee is a matter between Condor Trails and the Operator. It does not change who your trip contract is with.

2.2 Making a booking

Start with an enquiry to Condor Trails — by the forms on this site, email, or phone. We will come back to you in English with a written quote prepared with the Operator. A booking is confirmed only when the Operator has accepted it and sent you written confirmation. The person making the booking must be at least 18 years of age and accepts the Operator's booking conditions, as set out in the written quote, on behalf of all members of the travelling party.

Submitting an enquiry, opening an email from a form on this site, or receiving a quote does not create a booking and does not require you to pay anything to Condor Trails.

2.3 Deposit

Deposit and cancellation terms are set by our Peru operating partner and confirmed in your written quote. Any deposit is paid to the Operator, not to Condor Trails, on the instructions in that quote.

2.4 Balance payment

When the remaining balance is due, and how it should be paid, is set by the Operator and confirmed in your written quote. Balance payments are made to the Operator, not to Condor Trails.

2.5 Payment methods

Payment methods, currency, and any bank or conversion fees are set by the Operator and confirmed in your written quote. Condor Trails does not take trip payments, deposits, or balances on this website.

2.6 Late payment

If a payment to the Operator is late, the Operator's terms — as confirmed in your written quote — apply. Condor Trails cannot treat a booking as confirmed, cancelled, or refunded on the Operator's behalf except by passing on what the Operator has decided.

3. Pricing

Guide prices on this website are quoted in euro (EUR), per person unless otherwise stated, and are based on costs known at the time they were published. They are a starting point, not a confirmed offer. Ask us if you would like the equivalent quoted in US dollars (USD), pounds sterling (GBP), or Australian dollars (AUD). The price you pay is the one confirmed in your written quote.

Once your booking is confirmed with the Operator and any required deposit has been paid to them, the price is as stated in that confirmation, except for any later adjustment the Operator's terms allow. Those adjustment rules, including any rights you may have if the price changes, will be set out in your written quote.

4. Cancellation Policy

4.1 Cancellation by you

Deposit and cancellation terms are set by our Peru operating partner and confirmed in your written quote. If you need to cancel, notify Condor Trails in writing at info@condortrails.com as well as following any instructions in your quote, so we can help you communicate with the Operator in English.

Some elements of a trip may be non-refundable once reserved. Any such elements will be identified in your written quote where they are known at that time.

4.2 Cancellation by the Operator

If the Operator cancels your trip, the remedies available to you — such as an alternative arrangement or a refund of money you have paid to the Operator — are set by the Operator and confirmed in your written quote. Condor Trails will help you in English to understand and follow up those arrangements. Condor Trails does not itself hold your trip payments, so any refund of trip money is paid by the Operator.

5. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend that you take out comprehensive travel insurance before you travel, including cover for trip cancellation and curtailment, medical expenses and emergency evacuation (including high-altitude rescue where relevant), personal liability, luggage, and repatriation. If your itinerary includes trekking or other adventure activities, check that your policy covers them.

Whether the Operator requires insurance, and any details you must provide before departure, will be confirmed in your written quote. Condor Trails does not sell insurance and is not responsible for costs that arise if your cover is missing or inadequate.

6. Itinerary Changes

Travel in Latin America can be affected by weather, trail or road conditions, safety advice, government rules, or the availability of hotels, transport, guides, or permits. The Operator may need to change an itinerary before or during a trip. Alternatives and any refund or credit that may follow a significant change are set by the Operator and confirmed in your written quote.

Condor Trails will let you know in English as soon as we have been informed of a significant change, and will help you consider the options the Operator offers.

7. Liability

7.1 Our role

Condor Trails acts as an introducing agent. We sell on behalf of the Operator. We do not ourselves provide the hotels, transport, guides, permits, or other travel services that make up your trip, and we are not the organiser of those services under your booking with the Operator. Those services are provided under the Operator's contract with you.

7.2 Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Condor Trails is not liable for the performance of travel services supplied by the Operator or other providers, or for death, injury, illness, loss, damage, delay, or expense arising from their acts or omissions, from events beyond our reasonable control, or from your failure to follow safety instructions or local laws.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by Condor Trails' proven negligence, or any liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law, including Irish or EU law where it applies.

7.3 Package travel and consumer law

Because your trip contract is with the Operator in Peru, these website terms do not treat Condor Trails as the organiser of your package. Some consumer protections in Ireland, the European Union, or your home country may still be relevant, depending on how the booking is made. We will set out the position, as we understand it at the time, in your written quote. These terms are not legal advice, and they do not take away any right you cannot lawfully waive.

8. Health & Fitness

Many destinations involve high altitude, remote locations, and physically demanding activities. It is your responsibility to:

  • Ensure you are physically fit and healthy enough for your chosen itinerary
  • Disclose any medical conditions, allergies, dietary requirements, or mobility limitations before booking
  • Obtain appropriate vaccinations and medical advice for your destination countries
  • Carry necessary personal medications

The Operator may refuse participation in a specific activity if there is a risk to your safety or the safety of others. Any such rule will be applied by the Operator in line with your written quote.

9. Travel Documents

It is your responsibility to hold a valid passport (with at least six months' validity beyond your return date, where that is required) and any necessary visas, entry permits, or transit documents for all countries on your itinerary. Condor Trails can share general guidance in English, but we cannot accept liability for loss or expense if the correct documents are not in place.

10. Age Requirements

The lead person making a booking must be at least 18 years of age. Travellers under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all times during the trip, unless your written quote says otherwise. For certain activities (such as trekking routes or adventure sports), minimum age requirements set by the Operator or local authorities may apply. We will pass those on in your written quote when they are known.

11. Force Majeure and trips cut short

Neither Condor Trails nor, except as the Operator's own terms provide, the Operator can be treated as responsible for failure or delay caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control. That includes natural disasters, severe weather, epidemics, war or civil unrest, government travel advice or border closures, and strikes.

What happens if a trip is delayed, changed, or cut short — including any credit, rebooking, or refund — is set by our Peru operating partner and confirmed in your written quote. Condor Trails will help you in English to raise the matter with the Operator. We strongly recommend that your travel insurance covers cancellation and curtailment.

12. Complaints Procedure

If you have a complaint during your trip, please raise it at once with your guide or the Operator so it can be addressed on the ground. Please also tell Condor Trails, in English, so we can help you follow it up.

If you remain dissatisfied after you return, write to us within 28 days of your return date at:

13. Data Protection

We collect and process your personal data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law, including the GDPR and Irish data protection legislation where they apply. If you enquire or book, you understand that we will share the information needed to prepare a quote and fulfil a booking — which may include your name, contact details, passport data, and dietary or medical information — with the Operator in Peru.

These Terms & Conditions, which govern your use of this website and your dealings with Condor Trails as an introducing agent, are governed by the laws of the Republic of Ireland. The Irish courts have jurisdiction over disputes arising from these website terms, without limiting any mandatory rights you may have in your country of residence.

The law that governs your booking with the Operator, and the forum for disputes under that booking, will be confirmed in your written quote.
